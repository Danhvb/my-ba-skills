## Interview Agenda

### 1. Introduction (5 minutes)
- Introduce yourself and project objectives
- Explain interview purpose and how information will be used
- Request permission to record/take notes
- Confirm time availability

### 2. Background & Context (10 minutes)
**Questions**:
- Can you describe your role and key responsibilities?
- How long have you been in this role?
- What systems/tools do you currently use?
- Who do you interact with regularly in your work?

**Notes**:
[Space for notes]

### 3. Current State Understanding (15 minutes)
**Questions**:
- Can you walk me through your typical workflow for [specific process]?
- What are the main challenges or pain points you face?
- How much time do you spend on [specific activity]?
- What workarounds have you developed?

**Notes**:
[Space for notes]

### 4. Requirements & Needs (15 minutes)
**Questions**:
- What would an ideal solution look like for you?
- What features or capabilities are most important?
- What would make your job easier?
- Are there any must-have requirements?
- What nice-to-have features would you like?

**Notes**:
[Space for notes]

### 5. Constraints & Dependencies (5 minutes)
**Questions**:
- Are there any technical constraints we should be aware of?
- What systems must we integrate with?
- Are there regulatory or compliance requirements?
- What's your timeline expectation?

**Notes**:
[Space for notes]

### 6. Success Criteria (5 minutes)
**Questions**:
- How will you measure success of this project?
- What metrics are important to you?
- What would make this project a failure?

**Notes**:
[Space for notes]

### 7. Wrap-up (5 minutes)
- Summarize key points
- Clarify any ambiguities
- Identify any follow-up items
- Thank stakeholder for their time
